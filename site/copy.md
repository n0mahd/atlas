## Voice

Write as if the words will be cut in stone: steady, load-bearing, and certain. Lead with what holds, not what fails—use plain architectural verbs (*configure, hold, stand, carry, remain*) and full, balanced periods over chatty fragments or SaaS shorthand. No hype, no playfulness, no exclamation marks, and no buzzwords (*seamless, supercharge, unlock, effortless, magic*). Let the name carry its own weight; at most one restrained nod to bearing a load, never overt mythology.

## 1. Hero (29 words)

### Headline options (pick marked)

1. **`Configure once. Every AI agent, every machine.`** *(7 words — **PICK**)*  
   Two-beat engraved cadence suited to Cinzel capitals: leads with the promise, states both dimensions in full, and stays free of insider jargon.
2. `Configure your AI agents once, for every machine.` *(8 words)*  
   Speaks directly to the reader in plain prose, though a single trailing clause carries less architectural weight than a crisp break.
3. `One configuration. Every AI agent, every machine.` *(7 words)*  
   Frames Atlas as a single pillar rather than an action, though "configuration" is long to set cleanly in large display capitals.
4. `Configure once. Every harness, every machine.` *(6 words)*  
   Tightest rhythm and uses the owner's preferred term ("harness"), but drops "AI" and assumes cold readers know "harness" means coding agent.

### Decision on the supporting line

**Yes—the supporting line should now name drift.** With the headline leading on strength and promise, the supporting line needs to introduce the tension immediately: it names all five managed items, explains why a foundation is needed, anchors the `Without Atlas` drift grid right beside the hero, and lets the medallion and answer line land as a clear resolution rather than a restatement of the headline.

### Final hero copy

- **Headline** (7 words):  
  `Configure once. Every AI agent, every machine.`
- **Supporting line** (12 words):  
  `Without a foundation, skills, instructions, memory, hooks and MCP servers drift apart.`
- **Answer line** (10 words):  
  `Atlas holds one source in git and carries it everywhere.`
